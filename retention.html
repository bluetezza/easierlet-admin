Retention

Tenants approaching or past their data-retention deadline. Anonymisation runs nightly.
Pending retention actions
Items with `retention_expires_at` due in the next 30 days, not yet anonymised.
How this works
  • 30 days before: we email the landlord a warning. The tenant's record is still untouched.
  • On the due date: personal fields are nulled out, the auth account is deleted, and a retention.anonymised entry is logged. For rejected applicants, the row is deleted entirely.
  • Defer 30 days: push the deadline 30 days into the future and reset the warning. Use sparingly — needs a documented reason.
  • Force anonymise / delete: sets the deadline to right now so the next nightly job processes it within hours.
