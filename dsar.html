UK GDPR procedure reminder
  • Verify identity first. Don't generate a DSAR for an email you can't tie to the requester.
  • 30-day response window. Usually deliver within a few days; one-off extensions only when justified.
  • Review for third-party data. Check the export doesn't expose other users' personal data (e.g. landlord notes about a referee).
  • Deliver securely. The 24-hour signed URL is sent via Resend or shared with the requester directly. Don't email the PDF as an attachment to anyone other than the data subject.
